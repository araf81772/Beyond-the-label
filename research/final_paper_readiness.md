## Limits on the claims

- D is an allocation effect, not the fully-correct-pair proportion. Correct
  pairs were 57/64 separate and 55/64 inline.
- HIGH-STAKES optimality was 52/64 (81.25%), a narrow pooled pass. Its subgroup
  asymmetry is descriptive, not an additional confirmatory finding.
- Readbacks were independent branches; their accuracy does not establish a
  causal recognition-to-action pathway.
- First-action regret assumes optimal continuation. Two invalid first actions,
  sixteen invalid continuations and one missing continuation remain; only
  429/448 decisions have realised loss.
- The layout interaction interval includes zero. This establishes neither an
  inline benefit nor equivalence.
- Known cost and retained billing uncertainty are separate. All forty old
  reservations remain held; there was no new unknown charge or resampling.
